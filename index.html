    What the model does, and what it leaves out

    On every change

    1. Closed breakers split the network into islands. An island with no plant goes dead.
    2. Each island's plants share its load in proportion to capacity. If the plants are short, the island sheds load pro rata.
    3. A DC load flow (lossless lines, a fixed reactance per line) gives the MW on every closed line, compared with an invented rating.

    Left out

    Voltage, reactive power, frequency, transients and relay timing. Closing a breaker never checks synchronism. The two transformers are drawn but not modelled. The topology is a simplified ring, not the NGCP network, and nothing here comes from a real feed.
